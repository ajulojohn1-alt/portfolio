COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device when you visit a site. We keep our use of them minimal, essential, and transparent.

2. What we use

  • Essential (local storage): We store your cookie banner choice on your device so the prompt doesn't reappear on subsequent visits. This is strictly necessary and cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics (Microsoft Clarity): We use Microsoft Clarity to understand user interactions, scroll behavior, and heatmaps to improve site experience. Clarity uses first-party cookies (e.g., _clck, _clsk) to capture anonymised interaction metrics. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies via our cookie banner.
  • Embedded video (YouTube): Our walkthrough videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you click play. Clicking play may set YouTube cookies governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Advertising / targeting cookies: None.

3. Managing cookies

You can manage your consent preferences at any time using our cookie banner or clear cookies via your browser settings. Declining non-essential cookies disables Microsoft Clarity analytics without affecting core website navigation.

4. Changes

We may update this policy periodically to reflect technology updates or legal guidance. The date above shows the latest version.
