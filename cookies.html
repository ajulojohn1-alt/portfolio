COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We keep our use of them minimal.

2. What we use

  • Essential (local storage): we store your cookie-banner choice on your device so the banner doesn't reappear. This is strictly necessary and cannot be switched off.
  • Embedded video (YouTube): our walkthrough video uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only loads when you click the play button. Clicking it may set YouTube cookies, which are governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Advertising / tracking cookies: none.
  • Analytics: none currently. If we add analytics, we will use cookieless tools or ask for your consent first.

3. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you block all cookies, the cookie-banner choice will not be remembered. The video will simply not load until you click it.

4. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
