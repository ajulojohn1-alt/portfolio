Website and automation questions, answered

Why is this priced higher than typical freelancers?

You're buying agency-grade infrastructure, not a template with your logo on it: a bespoke build engineered for speed and conversion, no vulnerable plugins to maintain, and Concierge support after launch. Agencies delivering comparable quality typically charge £10k–£15k.

What is Concierge Care?

Concierge Care is our managed service, required with every website build. It covers unlimited updates, secure high-speed hosting, security monitoring and ongoing performance tracking, so you never chase a developer or worry about downtime.

Can I buy automations without a new website?

Yes. The Lead Rescue, Cash Flow Chaser, Reputation Loop and GEO AI Optimiser work with most existing setups, including Wix and WordPress. Tell us what you run and we'll confirm compatibility before you commit.

Do I own my website if I cancel Concierge Care?

Yes. You own the code and design. If you cancel, you can take the files and host them elsewhere, but you lose the updates, security monitoring and performance tracking that keep the site earning.

How much does a website cost for a UK service business?

With us, a website costs from £2,497 for a single-page build to £6,997 with a storefront, AI search optimisation and one automation, plus £49–£99 a month for Concierge Care. Agencies typically charge £10k–£15k for comparable builds.

Who do you build websites for?

UK service businesses of every kind. Removals and other trades are our deepest specialism — see the UK Trades page — and we also design for salons and beauty studios, solicitors, accountants and interior designers, with live concept demos of each in our folio.

What is missed call text back and does it work for my business?

Missed call text back is an automatic text sent to anyone whose call you miss, so the conversation continues before they contact a competitor. It suits any business that is hands-on during the day — on a roof, behind a chair or in a client meeting. Our Lead Rescue sends it through Twilio.

Is a Wix website bad for SEO?

No. A well-built Wix site can rank well, and our Joel Removals site is a custom Wix build. Static sites tend to load faster and need less security upkeep, which is why our core packages are static and Wix is offered from £7,997+ for owners who want to edit their own content.

How do I get my business recommended by ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI answers?

AI assistants favour businesses whose site says clearly what they do, where and for whom, marked up with structured data (JSON-LD schema) and backed by consistent details on Google Business Profile. Our GEO AI Optimiser adds that markup for £497. Nobody can guarantee an AI recommendation.

Do you guarantee Google rankings or a set number of leads?

No, and nobody honestly can. We build the fast, search-ready foundations and the enquiry system, but rankings and lead volume depend on competition, reviews, content and time.

Which trades do you build websites for?

Removals, man and van and house clearance are our strongest focus, backed by our live Joel Removals build — the UK Trades page has the full detail. Beyond trades we design for salons, solicitors, accountants and interior designers, working remotely across the UK.
