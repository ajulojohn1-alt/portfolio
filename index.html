// FAQS

COMMON QUESTIONS

Does the one-page site include a quote form?

Yes. Every build — including the one-page Quote Starter — includes a working quote/contact form that notifies you the moment a customer submits it, plus click-to-call.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We improve your visibility and enquiry setup, but rankings depend on competition, reviews, content, and time.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, photos, services, contact information, and any logo or reviews you already have.

How fast can you deliver?

First draft often within 48 hours after content received. Full launch usually within a few days depending on scope.

How does the referral scheme work?

Refer a tradesperson using the form above. If they become a paying client and go live, they get a discount and you get cash — £25 to £100 depending on the package. Full details in the Referral Terms.

Can I update the site myself?

For simple custom builds, I can help with updates. If you want full self-editing, we can discuss the best setup for you.

Do you work with trades outside removals?

Yes. Removals and local service trades are our strongest focus, but we can help other trades too.

Do you take payment upfront?

Yes. A deposit is required before work begins.

Can you fix my existing Wix site?

Yes, but that is quoted separately as a rescue/optimisation project.

Do you promise a certain number of leads?

No. We build a better enquiry system and improve your online presentation, but we do not guarantee a fixed number of leads.
