// FAQS

COMMON QUESTIONS

Does the Quote Starter System include a quote form?

Yes. The £350 single-page Quote Starter includes a working quote/contact form that notifies you the moment a customer submits it, plus click-to-call.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We improve your visibility, site speed, and enquiry setup, but rankings depend on competition, reviews, content, and time.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, photos, services, contact information, and any logo or reviews you already have.

How fast can you deliver?

First draft for the Quote Starter is often within 48 hours after content is received. Full launches usually take 3 to 7 business days.

Can I update the site myself?

For simple custom builds, I can help with updates. If you want full self-editing, the Custom Wix package is the best fit for you.

Do you work with trades outside removals?

Yes. Removals and local service trades are our strongest focus, but we can help other trades too.

Do you take payment upfront?

Yes. A deposit is required before work begins.

Can you fix my existing Wix site?

Yes, but that is quoted separately as a rescue/optimisation project.

Do you promise a certain number of leads?

No. We build a better, faster enquiry system and improve your online presentation, but we do not guarantee a fixed number of leads.
