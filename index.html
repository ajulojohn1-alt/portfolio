// RESPONSIVE BY DEFAULT

BUILT FOR THE PHONE IN THEIR HAND

Most people searching for a removals man, a roofer or a cleaner are on a phone — usually one-handed, usually in a hurry. That is the view we design for first. Desktop is the bonus, not the starting point.

Fully responsive design

Every layout reflows properly across phone, tablet and desktop. Nothing pinches, nothing overlaps, no sideways scrolling. Images compress and code stays lean so it loads fast on mobile data.

WhatsApp click-to-chat button

A WhatsApp button that opens a conversation with you in one tap — pre-filled with a message if you like. Customers who hate forms still get in touch, and you get the enquiry while the job is on their mind.

Click-to-call & sticky contact bar

Your number stays within thumb's reach as the customer scrolls, so "call now" is never a hunt. Tap-to-call works across every page and every package we build.

An adequate quote form — not a maze

Short fields, clear labels, sensible dropdowns for job type and postcode, and an instant email to you the moment it's submitted. Enough detail to quote from, short enough that people actually finish it.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What is the £350 Quote Starter System?

It's a one-page, quote-oriented website for trades — built mobile-first and responsive across phone, tablet and desktop. It includes a working quote form with instant email notification, click-to-call, a WhatsApp button, and on-page SEO basics with Google indexing. First draft in 48 hours, live within days. Founding client offer: the first 5 trade clients get it for £297 in exchange for a testimonial and case study use.

Does the one-page site include a quote form?

Yes. Every build — including the £350 one-page Quote Starter System — includes a working quote/contact form that notifies you the moment a customer submits it, plus click-to-call and a WhatsApp button.

Are the websites mobile responsive?

Yes. Everything we build is mobile-first and fully responsive across phone, tablet and desktop, with thumb-friendly buttons, WhatsApp click-to-chat and a quote form that fits on one screen. You can see the live mobile view of JoelRemovals.co.uk in the case study above.

Do you guarantee Google rankings?

No. We improve your visibility and enquiry setup, but rankings depend on competition, reviews, content, and time.

What do you need from me?

Your business details, photos, services, contact information, and any logo or reviews you already have.

How fast can you deliver?

First draft often within 48 hours after content received. Full launch usually within a few days depending on scope.

Can I update the site myself?

For simple custom builds, I can help with updates. If you want full self-editing, we can discuss the best setup for you.

Do you work with trades outside removals?

Yes. Removals and local service trades are our strongest focus, but we can help other trades too.

Do you take payment upfront?

Yes. A deposit is required before work begins.

Can you fix my existing Wix site?

Yes, but that is quoted separately as a rescue/optimisation project.

Do you promise a certain number of leads?

No. We build a better enquiry system and improve your online presentation, but we do not guarantee a fixed number of leads.
