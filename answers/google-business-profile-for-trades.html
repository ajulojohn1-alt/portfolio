How do I optimise my Google Business Profile as a tradesman?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · 5 min read

Quick Answer

Optimising your Google Business Profile (GBP) is the fastest way to get found locally. Ensure your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are consistent everywhere online, choose the most specific primary category (e.g., "Removal Service" not just "Business"), upload real photos of your vans and team, and actively request reviews from happy customers.

1. The NAP Consistency Rule

NAP stands for Name, Address, Phone number. Google cross-references this data across the web. If your Facebook page says "Joe's Removals" but your website says "Joe's Removals Ltd", Google gets confused and may lower your local ranking. Ensure it is exactly identical everywhere.

2. Categories and Services Matter

Don't just pick a broad category. If you are a removals company, select "Removal Service" as your primary category, and add secondary categories like "Man and Van Service" or "Storage Facility". Fill out the "Services" section with specific keywords (e.g., "Office Relocation", "Piano Moving").

3. The Review Flywheel

Reviews are the #1 ranking factor for the local "Map Pack". After every successful job, send the customer a direct link to your GBP review page. Respond to every review, good or bad, within 24 hours. When we managed this process for Joel Removals, consistently responding to reviews and keeping the profile updated with fresh photos significantly boosted our local visibility.
