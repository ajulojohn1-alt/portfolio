Do I need a website for my trade business?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · 4 min read

Quick Answer

Yes. While word-of-mouth is powerful, the vast majority of UK consumers now check a business online before contacting them. A simple, mobile-friendly website acts as your 24/7 salesperson, building immediate trust and capturing quote requests even when you are on the tools or unavailable.

The "Google Check" Reality

When a customer gets a recommendation for a plumber, removals company, or builder, what is the very first thing they do? They type the business name into Google. If nothing comes up, or if the only result is an outdated Facebook page, hesitation sets in. In the trades, no website often equals "risky" in the customer's mind.

Why a Facebook Page Isn't Enough

Facebook is great for community engagement, but it is not a professional storefront. It is cluttered with distractions, lacks proper SEO for local searches, and makes it difficult for customers to find your service areas, insurance details, or a dedicated quote form. A standalone website gives you complete control over the customer journey.

Real-World Proof: The Trust Factor

At Joel Removals, a business we have helped build and improve online for years, customers regularly tell us they chose us specifically because the website made the business feel established, transparent, and trustworthy. A clean, professional site answers the customer's unspoken question: "Are these people legitimate?" before they even pick up the phone.
