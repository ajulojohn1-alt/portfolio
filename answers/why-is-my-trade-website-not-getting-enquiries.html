Why is my trade website not getting enquiries?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · 4 min read

Quick Answer

If your trade website isn't getting enquiries, it is usually due to one of three things: it is not mobile-friendly, the contact method is hidden or complicated, or it lacks trust signals (like reviews, clear pricing, or insurance badges). Customers need to know you are legitimate and easy to reach within 5 seconds.

1. The Mobile Test

Open your website on your phone right now. Do you have to pinch and zoom to read the text? Is the phone number clickable? If yes, you are losing the majority of your potential customers. Google also penalises non-mobile-friendly sites in local search rankings.

2. The "Hunt for the Phone Number" Problem

If a customer has to scroll past three paragraphs of text or click through a "Contact Us" page just to find your number or a quote form, they will leave. Your primary call-to-action (a big "Get a Quote" button or a sticky click-to-call bar) must be visible immediately.

3. Missing Trust Signals

Trades rely heavily on trust. A site with no photos of your actual work, no customer reviews, and no mention of your service areas looks like a generic template—or worse, a scam. When we audited several outdated trade sites, simply adding a 3-field quote form and a "Fully Insured" badge doubled enquiry rates within a month.
