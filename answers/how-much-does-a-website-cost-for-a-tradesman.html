How much does a website cost for a tradesman?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · 5 min read

Quick Answer

A basic, professional one-page trade website in the UK typically costs between £150 and £400. A more comprehensive 4–6 page lead-generation site with advanced forms and local SEO setup usually ranges from £900 to £1,500. This covers design, mobile optimisation, a working quote form, and basic search engine setup.

What you actually get for your money

When you pay for a trade website, you aren't just buying a "digital brochure." You are buying a system designed to capture enquiries. A proper build includes:

  • Mobile-first design: Over 70% of trade searches happen on phones.
  • Working quote forms: That email you instantly when a customer submits them.
  • Click-to-call buttons: Removing friction for urgent jobs.
  • Trust signals: Review sections, insurance badges, and clear service areas.

The "One Extra Job" Rule

When we rebuilt the quote flow on JoelRemovals.co.uk (a real removals business we help run), the initial investment in a proper, mobile-friendly site paid for itself within the first three bookings. For most trades, a single medium-sized job covers the entire cost of a professional website. If your current site (or lack of one) is causing you to lose even one job a month to a competitor with a better online presence, it is already costing you more than a new build.

Why cheap DIY sites often cost more long-term

Free website builders seem attractive, but they often result in slow loading speeds, clunky mobile views, and hidden monthly subscription fees. More importantly, they rarely include proper local SEO foundations, meaning your business remains invisible on Google Maps. A professionally built, lightweight site avoids these traps and converts visitors into paying customers.
