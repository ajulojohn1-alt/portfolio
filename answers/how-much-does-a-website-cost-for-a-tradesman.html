Is a Wix or Squarespace site good enough for a tradesman?

Often yes, if you build it well. The platform matters less than the content: clear services, towns, reviews, a visible phone number and a short quote form. The common problems are slow, image-heavy pages and thin content. Google found 53% of mobile visits are abandoned if a page takes longer than three seconds (a 2016 study of over 10,000 mobile domains, still widely cited). Check any site, whatever it is built on, with Google's PageSpeed Insights.

What drives the price up?

  • Number of pages: one page per service and per town takes longer but ranks for more searches.
  • Custom design versus a proven template.
  • Bookings, payments or integrations with job-management software.
  • Who writes the copy and supplies photos.
  • Ongoing support, hosting and SEO work.

Related questions

How much does a plumber's website cost in the UK?

A simple plumber's site costs £2,497 as a single-page build with us, £1,000 to £3,000 from a freelancer for 5 to 10 pages, or £10 to £50 a month on a DIY builder. Cost rises with the number of service and town pages.

Can I get a decent website for under £500?

On a DIY builder, yes — you pay monthly and build it yourself. A bespoke, professionally built site with a quote form and click-to-call starts at £2,497 with us. What you are paying for is the copy, structure, speed and schema that turn visits into calls.

What are the hidden costs of a website?

Whito reports hidden costs add 30% to 50% to a quote, typically content, photography and extra pages. Also check domain renewal, hosting, email, and what updates cost after launch.

Is a cheap website worth it for a trade business?

It is if it loads quickly on a phone, shows your services, areas, reviews and accreditations, and has a working quote form. Price matters less than whether those basics are there.
