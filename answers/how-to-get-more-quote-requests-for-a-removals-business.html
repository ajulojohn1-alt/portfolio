How do I get more quote requests for my removals business?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · Updated 2 October 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: Put a short quote form (name, phone, collection and delivery postcode, move date) in the first screen on mobile, show insurance, reviews and trade memberships beneath the headline, build a page for each town you serve, and reply to every enquiry within minutes. UK completions run at around 100,000 a month and dip over the winter, so time your effort for the busy spring and summer months.

When do removals customers search?

Demand follows the housing market. HMRC publishes residential completions monthly on gov.uk, and the pattern is consistent: volume builds through spring, peaks over the summer, then drops over the winter. Check the latest monthly figures before you plan, and aim your website changes, Google Business Profile photos and review requests at the busy spring and summer completion period, not after it.

What should a removals quote form ask?

Every extra field loses some visitors, so ask only what you need to start the conversation and collect the rest on the call.

Which trust signals matter most for removals?

Customers are handing over everything they own, so risk is the main barrier. Put these directly under the headline: goods in transit and public liability insurance, British Association of Removers membership if you hold it, a clear statement of whether quotes are fixed or hourly, and real reviews with a first name and town. In BrightLocal's 2026 US survey, 41% of consumers always read reviews and 31% will only use businesses rated 4.5 or higher, so make the rating visible. When we optimised the layout for Joel Removals, placing trust badges beneath the main headline increased visitor confidence and direct form submissions.

Why does speed to reply win removals jobs?

Movers are often on a job when the phone rings. Moneypenny's 2016 report found 69% of callers won't leave a voicemail, and a j2 Global survey found over a third of UK callers would ring the next business. Reply within minutes, even if only by text. An automatic neutral reply to a missed call is legal under PECR when it contains no promotion.

Make sure the site loads quickly on a phone too: in Google's 2016 research, 53% of mobile visits are abandoned after three seconds.

Related questions

How do I get more removals leads online?

Combine a verified Google Business Profile, a fast mobile site with a short quote form, town pages for each area you cover, and fast replies. Each fixes a different leak between search and booking.

What should a removals quote form include?

Name, mobile, collection postcode, delivery postcode and move date. Collect stairs, parking and inventory on the follow-up call.

Should a removals website show prices?

Showing ranges or what affects the price builds trust and helps you rank for cost searches. You don't need to publish a fixed price list, but say whether quotes are fixed or hourly.

How do I rank for 'man and van near me'?

Verify a Google Business Profile with the right category and service areas, collect recent reviews that mention the town, and build a dedicated page for each main area with unique local detail.
