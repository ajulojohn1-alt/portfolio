How do I get more quote requests for my removals business?

Written by John at Ajulo Web Solutions · 5 min read

Quick Answer

To get more quote requests, your removals website must have a prominent, simple quote form above the fold, clear service area lists, and visible trust signals (reviews, insurance badges). Most importantly, ensure your site loads instantly on mobile, as the majority of customers search for movers on their phones.

1. The 3-Second Mobile Rule

If a customer lands on your site from their phone and cannot immediately see a phone number or a quote form without scrolling, they will bounce to the next removals company on Google. Your primary call-to-action must be visible instantly.

2. Reduce Friction in Your Quote Form

Do not ask for 15 fields of information. A high-converting removals quote form only needs: Collection postcode, Delivery postcode, and a phone number/email. You can get the rest of the details (e.g., number of bedrooms, stairs) on the follow-up call. Every extra field you add drops your conversion rate.

3. Display Trust Signals Prominently

Moving is stressful and high-risk for customers. They need to know you are legitimate. Display "Fully Insured," "No Hidden Fees," and real customer reviews (with first names and towns) directly on the homepage. When we optimised the layout for JoelRemovals.co.uk, placing these trust badges directly beneath the main headline noticeably increased the confidence of visitors and boosted direct form submissions.
