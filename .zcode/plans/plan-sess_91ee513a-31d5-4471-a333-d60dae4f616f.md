Move the current trades-focused homepage content to `/trades` (trimmed into a focused landing page), build a new vertical-neutral homepage at `/`, and keep every cross-page link, SEO surface and the live case study working.

## 1. Create `/trades` (new `trades.html`, served at `/trades`)
- Copy `index.html` as the base, then trim to a focused trades page:
  - **Keep**: the trades hero (rewritten to name the audience), the Joel Removals proof section, the packages/pricing section, the automations section, trade FAQs, contact form, and nav/footer.
  - **Change**: hero headline/copy becomes trade-specific ("Websites and automations for UK trades and removals firms…"); remove or soften generic framing that belongs on the new homepage; FAQ answers that reference "who we work with" stay as-is.
  - **SEO**: canonical/og:url → `https://ajulowebsolutions.com/trades`, unique title ("Websites & automations for UK trades and removals…" with prices), update its JSON-LD (keep LocalBusiness `@id` anchors consistent with homepage schema so they merge, add `url: /trades`), add to `sitemap.xml` with fresh lastmod.
- Internal links inside `/trades` that pointed at homepage anchors (e.g. `#packages`) become same-page anchors.
