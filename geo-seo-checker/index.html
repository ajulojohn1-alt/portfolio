Plain-English guide

What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation, explained for busy trade owners. No jargon, no fluff.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) means building your website so AI assistants — ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews — can read it, trust it and recommend your business when a local customer asks. SEO gets you into the list of results. GEO gets you named inside the answer itself.

Here's the shift. When a homeowner types "roofer south london" into Google, they get a list of links and pick one. When they ask ChatGPT or Google's AI "who's a reliable roofer in South London?", the AI writes the answer and names a handful of businesses it trusts. If your facts are clear, consistent and structured, you're quotable. If they're vague, you're invisible — however good your work is.

One honest caveat: nobody can guarantee an AI recommendation, and anyone who promises one is selling snake oil. What you can do is remove every reason for an AI engine to skip you. That's what the five factors below do.

The five things AI assistants look for before recommending a trade

  1. Machine-readable facts. JSON-LD schema that states plainly who you are, what you do and where you work. It tells Google and every AI model the same story about your business.
  2. Consistent NAP. The same name, address and phone number on your website, Google Business Profile and directories. Contradictions make engines distrust every version.
  3. Plain service and area statements. "Roof repairs across South London" beats "excellence since 1998". AI quotes specifics, not slogans.
  4. Reviews and reputation. Google reviews and trusted directory listings feed the assistants that summarise local options.
  5. A fast, mobile-first site. The experience signals that Google rankings and AI crawlers both reward.

Why doesn't my trade website show up on Google?

Usually a combination of missing structured data, an unverified or incomplete Google Business Profile, inconsistent name, address and phone details, thin content and a slow mobile experience. Run the checker to see which apply to your site today.

How long does local SEO take to work for a small trade business?

Realistically three to six months for movement in competitive areas, though technical fixes can show results within weeks. AI citations follow a similar pattern: engines need to read consistent facts about you repeatedly before they trust them.

Three moves, in order: complete and verify your Google Business Profile, add LocalBusiness schema markup to your homepage, and state your service and coverage area in plain words with a tap-to-call phone number. Those three fix the biggest visibility leaks for most trades.
