PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Applies to ajulowebsolutions.com

1. Who we are

Ajulo Web Solutions ("we", "us") is a UK-focused web design studio operated remotely from the Philippines. Contact: john@ajulowebsolutions.com. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiry form: name, business name, trade type, email, phone, and your message.
  • Referral form: your name, your email, and the details of the business you refer.
  • Email correspondence: any information you send us by email.
  • Client project data: business details, logos, photos, and reviews you provide for your build.

3. Why we use it (lawful bases)

  • Consent: you tick the consent box on our forms; you can withdraw it at any time by emailing us.
  • Contract: to deliver services you request (building your website, paying referral bounties).
  • Legitimate interests: responding to enquiries, administering our referral programme, and keeping basic business records.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. It is only shared with service providers needed to operate: Formspree (form delivery), our hosting provider (Cloudflare), and payment processors (e.g., Wise/PayPal) when paying referral bounties or receiving payments.

5. International transfers

We operate from the Philippines, so enquiry and client data may be processed there. We keep the data we collect minimal and use reputable providers with appropriate security. By submitting our forms, you acknowledge this processing arrangement.

6. How long we keep it

  • Prospect/enquiry data we never convert: up to 12 months.
  • Client records (invoices, agreements): as long as required for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Referral records: until the bounty is settled plus reasonable record-keeping time.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your data, and you may object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Email john@ajulowebsolutions.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or the Philippine National Privacy Commission.
