2. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • You may not refer yourself, a business you own, or your spouse/partner.
  • Employees or contractors of Ajulo Web Solutions are not eligible.

3. Qualifying referrals

  • The referred business must be a new client we have not previously contacted or worked with.
  • If two people refer the same business, the bounty goes to the first referral received.
  • A bounty is only earned when the referred client's final balance has cleared and their project goes live.

4. Payment of bounties

  • Bounties are paid within 7 days of the referred client's final payment clearing.
  • Payment is made via Wise, PayPal, or bank transfer.
  • If you are an existing client, you may choose cash or double value as credit toward future work.
  • Discounts cannot be combined with the founding client offer or any other promotion; the client receives whichever single discount is greater.

6. Voiding and clawback

If the referred client cancels, demands a refund, or disputes payment, the bounty is void. If a bounty was paid and is later found to be fraudulent or ineligible, we may reclaim it.

7. Changes and termination

We may modify, pause, or end this programme at any time. Bounties already earned under these terms will be honoured.
